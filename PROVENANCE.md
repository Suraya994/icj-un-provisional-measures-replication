# Provenance

## Published substantive coding

All substantive legal and institutional coding underlying the published paper was conducted manually by **Suraya Bazarova**, the sole author. `primary_coder=Suraya Bazarova` and `primary_coding_mode=HUMAN_MANUAL` are retained in the 233-pair canonical file. Automated tools did not serve as substantive coders.

No independent second-coder dataset forms part of the published analysis. Public row-level files deliberately do not name a pending second coder. If independent revalidation is later completed, it must be released as a separate, dated post-publication dataset with its own scope and agreement statistics.

## Screening chronology

1. 41 ICJ decisions and 13,692 structured UN events define 561,372 theoretical combinations.
2. ±180-day date eligibility yields 17,413 pairs.
3. The original case-file dictionary screen operates on nonnegative-lag pairs and yields 233 preliminary code-9 candidates requiring full-document review. The historical dictionary and script have been restored in this public release; the portable reconstruction matches the published 233 keys exactly.
4. Those 233 pairs were manually coded by the author.
5. A later V8 evidence re-screen identified 120 additional E1–E4 candidate pairs outside the original 233. The published paper reports that the author opened/read all 120 and found no additional positive. Candidate IDs can be reconstructed. A 120-row binary human-review ledger is also preserved in the system and is released in reconstructed form, combining those exact candidate IDs with the manuscript-supported facts that all 120 were reviewed by Suraya Bazarova and none yielded an additional positive. The original four-way row-to-subcategory mapping is not preserved.
6. A/78/PV.59 was found outside the 13,692/17,413 upstream event universe and added as a fourth positive documentary record. It does not create a third positive order.

## What is frozen vs enriched

The published 41/13,692/17,413/233 coding architecture and published order-level outcomes are preserved. `data/07_postpublication_diagnostics` contains clearly marked later diagnostics: review coverage, case-level uncertainty, descriptive Fisher calculation and a partial Article 41(2) transmission registry. They are not represented as analyses performed before publication.

## Post-publication audit boundary

Files under `data/07_postpublication_diagnostics/` were created after publication to evaluate coverage, clustering, exposure documentation and screening behavior. They do not change the historical fact that the published substantive coding was performed by the sole author. No diagnostic file is presented as if it had been part of the original conference-paper workflow.
