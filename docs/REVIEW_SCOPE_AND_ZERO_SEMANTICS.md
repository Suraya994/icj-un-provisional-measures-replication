# Review scope and the meaning of zero

The published order-level indicator is a **screened-evidence indicator**, not an exhaustive census of every temporally eligible United Nations record.

For the 24 primary orders, 4,868 order-event pairs fall in the nonnegative 0–180 day window. The original 233 manual-review layer contains 209 of those primary post-order pairs. The reconstructed 120-candidate recall set adds preserved review evidence for 33 additional primary post-order pairs, giving 242/4,868 pairs with preserved review evidence in the public package.

Therefore:

- `1` = verified order-specific engagement exists.
- `0` = no order-specific positive was identified among the screened/reviewed candidate evidence available to the published workflow.
- `0` **does not mean** every eligible UN record was manually read and found negative.
- Records with no preserved manual-review evidence are explicitly labelled `NO_PRESERVED_MANUAL_REVIEW_EVIDENCE` in `PRIMARY_POSTORDER_4868_REVIEW_COVERAGE.csv`.

This distinction is essential. It prevents an unreviewed record from being represented as a substantively coded negative. The published paper itself states that the 233-pair layer is a screened review set and not an exhaustive adjudication of all 17,413 eligible pairs.
